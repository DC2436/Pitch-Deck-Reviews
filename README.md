# Pitch Deck Reviews

## Overview

Pitch Deck Reviews is a Product Management prototype designed for PitchSap that helps startup founders evaluate and improve their pitch decks through AI-powered analysis or expert consultant reviews.

The feature provides an end-to-end review workflow, allowing entrepreneurs to upload their pitch deck, receive structured feedback, identify improvement areas, and connect with experienced startup consultants for personalized guidance.

---

## Problem Statement

Many entrepreneurs struggle to determine whether their pitch deck is investor-ready before approaching investors.

Traditional feedback methods are often:
- Expensive
- Time-consuming
- Difficult to access
- Unstructured

This feature addresses these challenges by combining instant AI-powered analysis with optional expert reviews within a single platform.

---

## Solution

The Pitch Deck Reviews feature enables founders to:

- Upload pitch decks in PDF, PPT, or PPTX format
- Receive AI-generated analysis within minutes
- Review detailed startup evaluation reports
- Identify strengths and improvement areas
- Connect with experienced startup consultants
- Book one-on-one consultation sessions
- Track expert review progress
- Improve presentations based on actionable recommendations
